A place, reconstructed.

This pass focuses on Lorong 11. Hotel 81 Joy, Agape Centre and Hok Tek Chi Loke Yah Teng have reference-led exteriors. Heritage textures are AI reconstructions of public reference photographs, with estimated dimensions and some inferred details.

Lok Fu Lala Pot’s address is sourced; its exterior remains illustrative. Agape’s three-unit extent is provisional because historic, current and mapped address numbering differs. Other buildings remain approximate, and Lorong 13 is retained as background context.

Road centre lines and one-way tags come from OpenStreetMap, projected into metres. Widths, pavements, heights and props are estimates. No affiliation or endorsement by the depicted organisations is implied.
