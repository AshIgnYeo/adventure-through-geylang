BUILDING THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

A place, reconstructed.

Road centre lines and one-way tags come from OpenStreetMap, projected into metres. Road widths, pavements, façades and building heights are approximate. Business names are fictional.

The heritage frontage draws on URA’s descriptions of the early shophouses between Lorongs 11 and 13. Individual façades are illustrative, not surveyed replicas.

This is an exploration prototype. Multiplayer, encounters and a deadline will follow.
