# Gems Ville, 8 Lorong 13 Geylang

Research and implementation: **10 October 2026**. Open question 6, an authored footprint for the redeveloped lot.

## Evidence and assignment

- **Listings:** Google Maps lists **Gems Ville**, a condominium, at **8 Lor 13 Geylang**. Listing records describe a seven-storey, 24-unit boutique development with a brise-soleil façade and generous balconies, on a **731 m²** (7,868 sq ft) lot, completed in 2025 by East Asia Geylang Development ([PropertyGuru](https://www.propertyguru.com.sg/listing/for-sale-gems-ville-500169325)).
- **Street View, April 2024:** panoramas `8U4Z…`, `HaY-…`, `uh9Y…` and `f-IH…` show the nearly finished building, with a boom lift still at work. It has:
  - **GEMS VILLE** lettering on a dark podium;
  - bronze-toned vertical fins framing balcony bays with grey railings on six residential storeys;
  - a deep louvre screen at the top;
  - a slatted gate and a planted strip behind the front fence.

## The footprint

- **Replaced way:** source way [452646018](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/452646018), tagged 6A, 23.3 × 15.3 m, predates the redevelopment.
- **Frontage:** rectified on the old source front, the building's north edge drifts with the camera in a way that places the façade about **1 m behind** that line, and about 2.2 m north of the old way. Its south edge meets the source line of No. 4 (Sui Yuan Ju). That gives a **27.4 m** frontage.
- **Depth:** the depth, **26 m**, follows from the 731 m² lot. The registered satellite imagery predates completion, so it cannot confirm the rear.
- **Authored footprint:** `l13-8`.

## Elevation (approximate)

- **Podium:** dark, to 5.6 m.
- **Floor slabs:** at about 5.85, 8.7, 11.5, 14.35, 17.2 and 20.1 m, about 2.85 m apart, with the top storey to 22.6 m.
- **Louvre screen:** 22.6–26.3 m.
- **Fins:** seven bronze fins along the frontage.

Heights carry a few per cent of uncertainty from the depth correction. Render-and-compare from the `uh9Y…` pose puts the slabs, balconies, south edge and fence close to the photograph.

## Omitted

The boom lift, the rooftop, the entrance details and the interiors. Use `?review=gems-ville`. All **174 tests**, the type check and the build passed, with day and night renders and no console errors.
