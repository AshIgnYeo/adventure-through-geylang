# Geylang Road, south side, No. 352 to No. 364

Research and implementation from **14 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 86. This brief covers `src/geylang-352-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** the seven houses on Geylang Road's south side east of Lorong 18's east corner (No. 350, modelled with Lorong 18), from No. 352 to No. 364 at Lorong 20's west corner. Question 71 held them for a Lorong 20 walk, but Lorong 20 lies just outside the extract (question 81), so they are walked now (question 86). No. 364's Lorong 20 face is left for that walk.
- **Map:** `scripts/prepare-map.mjs` takes the seven buildings by id with Geylang Road's two ways past them, and a corridor carries Geylang Road on from the Lorong 18 link to past No. 364.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's westbound drive of 16 May 2023 (sequence 8344809), as on Sims Avenue's north side ([Sims Avenue north brief](sims-avenue-north-brief.md), which describes the method). Frames 3225 and 3228 are solved from the row's party walls with residuals of 0.27° and 0.44°; with them, the photographed party walls stand within 0.3 m of the source ones. Their positions sat 7.8-10.5 m west of their GPS fixes: on this westbound drive the fix trails behind.
- **Heights:** the cameras ride about 1.75 m above the five-foot-way floor. Heights are read above that floor and carry about ±0.4 m.
- **Naming:** every house is unnamed for now and its boards are drawn blank, since listings cannot be checked from here (open question 84). Their sign texts are recorded below.
- **Footprints:** every party wall slants about 0.13 m inward for every metre back from the front, so the five-foot way's shell and the east pier stand in from it. The outlines are kept.

## Status, 14 November 2026

**Complete.** All seven houses are modelled as measured elevations on their source fronts.
