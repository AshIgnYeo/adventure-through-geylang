## Masjid Haji Mohd Salleh, 245 Geylang Road

Research and direct visual inspection: **1 October 2026**. The [MUIS mosque directory](https://www.muis.gov.sg/community/mosque/mosque-directory/haji-mohd-salleh--g/) confirms the mosque name, **245 Geylang Road, Singapore 389304**, and states that the present building opened in November 1999 after rebuilding. MUIS describes it as a four-storey building with simple modern architecture. The retained [OpenStreetMap way 454254204](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/454254204) independently carries the same name, number, road, postcode and mosque use, with a three-level tag. The source footprint is therefore a high-confidence identity and placement match, while the floor-count difference remains unresolved.

The model follows the directly inspected [December 2020 CC BY-SA 4.0 photograph by Dekoelie](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Masjid_Haji_Mohd_Salleh.jpg) and the [LearnIslam exterior](https://learnislam.sg/mosque/haji-mohd-salleh-mosque-geylang/). These show a cream street elevation with deep green side screens, a broad central entrance arch, four upper window bays, twin square corner towers, circular motifs and a triangular central gable. The source polygon's **22.46 m** southern edge is used as the Geylang Road frontage. The main wall is estimated at **11.35 m**, with simplified tower and gable elements rising to approximately **13.6 m**. These values are visual estimates, not surveyed dimensions.

The reconstruction uses original code-native geometry and an original text panel. Circular tower ornament is abstracted, Arabic/Jawi text too small to transcribe reliably is represented by a neutral gold band, and the roof finials are simplified. Openings are opaque and no prayer hall, classrooms, worshippers, activities, temporary decorations or adjoining uses are reconstructed. The 2020 photograph is used as visual evidence under its licence but is not bundled or copied into the project. The LearnIslam image date is not stated. Current October 2026 appearance, exact internal storey division and unseen elevations remain unverified. Review: `?review=haji-mohd-salleh-mosque`.
