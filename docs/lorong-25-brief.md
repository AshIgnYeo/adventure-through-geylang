# Lorong 25

Research and implementation from **28 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 105. This brief covers `src/lorong-25-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Lorong 25 from Geylang Road to Sims Avenue, both sides, with the Geylang Road row between Aljunied Road and the lane and the corner block between Lorongs 25 and 25A: 26 outlines.
- **Source:** a narrow dated extract, `public/osm-extension-lorong-25.osm` (103.8822-103.8834 E, 1.3131-1.3158 N, retrieved 28 November 2026), read after the earlier extracts, so each earlier file keeps its versions. Four plots at the Geylang Road end were already held by the east extension, which never took them in. The lane's thirteen ways, four Geylang Road ways back to Aljunied Road and three Sims Avenue ways are taken by id, with three walkable corridors: the lane, Geylang Road from Lorong 25A to Aljunied Road, and Sims Avenue from Aljunied Road to Lorong 25A.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drive of 16 May 2023 (sequence 8344809): frames 2021-2047 up the lane and 556-571 west along Geylang Road.
- **Modelling:** the terrace of Nos. 6-26 house by house on its outlines; every other plot as photographed massing, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±2 m (±4 m for five storeys or more). The terrace's outlines run off square to the curving lane, so each house's five-foot way is inset from its party walls by what its outline needs, from 0.25 m at the north end to 1.1 m at No. 6.
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (question 84), its boards drawn blank. Signs and source names are recorded below with what the frames show. The source tags Nos. 14, 16, 20 and 22 to Lorong 15, a slip for Lorong 25.

## Status, 28 November 2026

**Complete.** Lorong 25A, between this lane's east side and the next block, is the next walk (open question 107).
