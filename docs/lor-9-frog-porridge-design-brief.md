Lettering is newly rendered from the observed place name in English and Chinese, with an approximate typeface and layout; the operator logo, food images, menus, contact details and other small text are omitted.

The retained Lorong 9 centreline and the existing **6.5 m estimated road width** overlap this source footprint, by about **2.23 m** at the long wall midpoint. This is a pre-existing source/width inconsistency. Neither the road centreline nor building outline is moved to disguise it. All new ground-level furniture, floor, piers and canopy stay within the source outline; sparse tables occupy the recessed public-facing edge. The visible lane width and setback therefore remain limited by the underlying map. A future road/footprint reconciliation requires better survey evidence and should be a separate task.
