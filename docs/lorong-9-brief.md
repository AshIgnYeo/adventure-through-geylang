# Lorong 9 Geylang

Research and implementation: **10 October 2026**. This brief covers `src/lorong-9-layout.mjs`.

## Depth

Three June 2024 panoramas (`TWmJ…`, `CCVS…` and `N-T1…`, then `hv2-…` and `d9Up…`) agree on these street fronts only about **4 m behind** the source front line. The same holds for No. 12, for both party lines of the grey building and for YWAM's party line. That is consistent with a position error on this drive, which the earlier PFCS brief also suspected ("draws the generic row about 13 m out instead of 7 m"). The models keep the source front and were measured on frames rectified 4 m back. Render-and-compare from poses moved 4 m back towards the road reproduces the photographs.

Lorong 9 is outside the derived road set, so the frames face west onto open ground in the model.

## The buildings

- **No. 12** (way 1223603459, unnamed): a weathered white three-storey building of the 1960s–70s. It has cantilevered floor slabs, a deep roof slab, banded glazing and grilles, yellow blinds, and an open car porch behind gates.
- **Nos. 8 and 6** (ways 1223603458 and 1223603457, unnamed): a recent eight-storey building in grey concrete panels with glazed bays under black louvre hoods, a tall slit stair window and a garden fence. Triangulation puts its party lines on the source lines. One elevation is laid out across both ways and sliced into each.
- **YWAM Singapore, No. 4** (way 453797939, tagged No. 4 with five levels): Google Maps lists Youth With A Mission Singapore at **8** Lor 9 Geylang. Its own brown plaque beside the door reads **YWAM SINGAPORE, 4 LORONG 9 GEYLANG**, on the yellow five-storey building. The plaque, the footprint tag and the frontage agree, so the signed occupant is modelled under open question 12, with the listing's No. 8 recorded. The building has cantilevered upper floors with balcony ledges over a recessed ground floor with a breezeblock panel.

Heights carry about ±1 m: the near frames are oblique, and the grey building's top is out of frame (estimated at eight storeys, 25 m).

Use `?review=l9-12`, `?review=l9-6-8` and `?review=ywam-singapore`.

## The west side

These ways straddle the study's west edge. Heights come from roofline elevations in June 2024 Lorong 9 panoramas and carry about ±2 m.

- **Kim Court, No. 11** (way 453797935, named): Google Maps lists Kim Court at 11 Lor 9 Geylang. KIM COURT lettering over the plate **11** stands on the pale entrance pillar of the yellow eight-storey apartment block. Triangulating the pillar from `CCVS…` and `TWmJ…` puts it 2.2 m behind the front line of the way tagged No. 11. The 10 October lock had placed the lettering on the way tagged 17, which was wrong. The block has stacks of curved balconies framed in maroon, a pale stair core and a car porch behind red gates, with its roof at about 24.7 m.
- **The Diamond, No. 17** (way 453797934, named): listed at 17 Lor 9 Geylang, with THE DIAMOND lettering over its car porch. A cream block with green trim, bay windows and diamond-lattice balustrades.
- **No. 7A** (way 453797930, unnamed): a weathered white four-storey block with ribbon windows, a timber-screened top floor and a glazed stair tower.
- **Nos. 3 and 5** (ways 453797937 and 453797936): construction sites in June 2024, with white corrugated hoarding painted No. 3, under scaffolding hung with sound-barrier sheeting about 8 m high. The Ji Yang Cai Clan Association (No. 3) and the Pu Guang Tang Buddhist Society (No. 5) are listed there. What is being built is not known, so the sites are shown as photographed, unnamed.

Use `?review=kim-court`, `?review=the-diamond` and `?review=l9-7a`.
