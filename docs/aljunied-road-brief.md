# Aljunied Road

Research and implementation from **28 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 102. This brief covers `src/aljunied-road-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Aljunied Road from Geylang Road north to the Lorong 23 coffee shop: nine plots on its west side, behind Lorong 23's east side, and three office blocks on its east side, with both carriageways and a corridor along each.
- **Source:** the dated extension `public/osm-extension.osm` (question 81), taken in by id. The east side's two northern blocks straddle the extension's east edge; their source outlines are whole.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drives of 15 and 16 May 2023 north along the road (sequences 8344585, frames 4999-5018, and 8344809, frames 1066-1082), matched to the plots by their order along the road.
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±2 m (±4 m for the office blocks). The ornate pair, No. 35 and the fruit shop stand within 7 m of the carriageway's centreline, so their awnings are held flat.
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (question 84); legible signs and source names are recorded below.
