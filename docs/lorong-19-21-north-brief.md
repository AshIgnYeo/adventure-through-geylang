# Lorongs 19 and 21 north of Sims Avenue

Research and implementation from **14 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 88. This brief covers `src/lorong-19-21-north-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Lorong 19 from Sims Avenue north to the extract's north edge, the road joining it to Lorong 21 (named Lorong 19 Geylang in the source), and Lorong 21 from the avenue to just past the extract's north edge, far enough to take in the front of No. 51, whose outline straddles it. Eleven outlines stand along them inside the extract.
- **Map:** `scripts/prepare-map.mjs` takes the eleven buildings and twelve ways by id; the two lanes and the joining road are walkable corridors.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drive of 16 May 2023 (sequence 8344809, frames 3670-3725), which runs up Lorong 19, back down it, along the joining road and up and down Lorong 21. No frame shows party walls, so positions are the GPS fix moved about 10 m along the drive, as on Lorong 17.
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, as on Lorong 17 north: colours, storeys, roofs and the main openings as photographed, with window rows placed by estimate. Heights follow storey counts and rooflines and carry about ±2 m, ±4 m for the towers.
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now, since listings cannot be checked from here (open question 84). Legible signs and source names are recorded below.

## Status, 14 November 2026

**Complete.** All eleven plots are modelled as massing. One footprint question is logged under [question 88](open-questions.md): No. 74's source front lies inside the joining road's modelled carriageway.
