# Sims Avenue south row infill

Research and implementation: **10 October 2026**. This brief covers the places in `src/sims-south-layout.mjs`. Heights are above the five-foot-way floor.

## Yap Sun Hardware, 120 Sims Avenue (dated exterior)

- **Signage and listing:** June 2024 Street View shows a blue awning lettered YAP SUN HARDWARE over a glazed hardware shop, on the source way tagged No. 120. Its neighbours are FSL EW LED Lighting (No. 118) and Thye Seng (No. 122). Google Maps lists Yap Sun Hardware Mfy at 105 Sims Ave, #04-09 Chancerlodge Complex.
- **Decision:** under open question 12, the signed shop is a dated exterior. It settles the 10 October lock and claims no current occupancy.
- **Depth:** read on `2Ajo…` and `u8Gt…`, rectified 3.25 m in front of the source front, the depth found at FSL and Thye Seng. The source front is kept, as for its neighbours (open question 9).
- **Elevation:** the upper storeys share FSL's three-storey type. On the same five-foot-way datum, their windows and vents fall at FSL's heights, so FSL's vertical elevation is used with No. 120's own window positions: two windows in tan surrounds on each floor, under louvred vents.

## No. 106 Sims Avenue (unnamed)

- **Footprint and identity:** the re-spaced No. 106 footprint (open question 8a), between Fok Wai Kee and Red Dot Running. Amrise Foodstuffs is listed at No. 106, but no sign was seen, so it is unnamed.
- **Elevation:** the same pale-pink row as Red Dot and Amrise. Each upper floor has four narrow windows and a pair under a long hood, each under its own red-brown hood, over a roller shutter, a timber door and an open shop.
- **Measurement:** two panoramas rectified at the row's 1.5 m depth disagree with Red Dot's storey spacing by about 17%. Red Dot's heights are kept so the row is continuous, and No. 106's window positions are scaled by the same factor. Positions carry about ±0.3 m.
