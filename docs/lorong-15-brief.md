# Lorong 15 Geylang: Sin Chew Tan's Clan Association and the Teck Keow Association

Research and implementation: **10 October 2026**. Open question 15, answered "source front, labelled". This brief covers `src/lorong-15-layout.mjs`.

## Depth and the split

- **Depth:** the April 2024 panoramas `9_3n…` and `tdl8…` agree on these frontages only on a plane about **4.8 m in front of** the source block, close to the modelled Lorong 15 carriageway. Under the owner's answer, both houses stand on the source front.
- **Measurement:** widths and heights were read on frames rectified at the photographed depth. There, Sin Chew Tan's house measures about 5.4 m and Teck Keow's about 5.1 m.
- **Footprints:** the northern 13 m strip of block 454254281, `l15-north`, is split at 6.6 m from its north end, in the ratio of those widths. The result is `l15-28a` and `l15-26a`, with cut lines square to the front.

## Sin Chew Tan's Clan Association 星洲陳氏公會, 28A

- **Identity:** a black board reading 星洲陳氏公會 SIN CHEW TAN'S CLAN ASSOCIATION, numbered 28A. No published address for the association was found; the owner approved building it on the signboard.
- **Elevation:** a white three-storey house with a band of dark glazing on the second storey, an open top loggia and a parapet at about 9.75 m.
- **Ground floor:** the Ganh Xua restaurant (Ganh Xua by Homely Vietnam, listed at 28 Lor 15, temporarily closed), with its lettering and a red awning.

## Singapore Teck Keow Association 新加坡德僑社, 26A

- **Identity:** listed by the SFCCA at 26A Lorong 15. Its white board reads 新加坡德僑社 SINGAPORE TECK KEOW ASSOCIATION.
- **Elevation:** a weathered three-storey house: dark glazing on the second storey, then 王氏慈善福樓 lettering under an ornamental grille on the top storey, at about 9.9 m.
- **Ground floor:** the 昱歡美食閣 eating-house board has no listing and is left plain.

## South of the hotel: Hong Sheng Koon, the Ship-Chandlers and No. 454254268

- **Depth:** triangulating the hotel's south edge and the next party downpipe from `4VkQ…` and `dwar…` puts this stretch about **4.4 m in front of** the source block. Three panoramas agree at that depth. It gets the same treatment as open question 15: the southern 14.5 m strip, `l15-south`, is split at the measured party lines, shifted onto the hotel's line, into three strips.
  - `l15-20e`, 6.4 m: **Hong Sheng Koon Chinese Koontow & Lion Dance Society 鴻勝館**. It is listed at 20E Lor 15, and its yellow vertical 鴻勝館 sign hangs on the top storey. It is a cream four-storey house with two windows on each upper floor under long louvred vents, over a recessed ground floor with a diamond-vent band.
  - `l15-20`, 6.8 m: **Singapore Ship-Chandlers Association**. It is listed at 20 Lor 15 (ssca.sg), with its knotted-rope board on a weathered cream four-storey house.
  - `l15-shed`, 1.3 m: an unnamed single-storey lean-to.
- **No. 454254268:** the source way south of the block. It holds a cream two-storey building with a stepped parapet and a blue shade over four upper windows. No listing matched it, so it is unnamed. Its photographed frontage spans the source way at the same 4.4 m depth.
- **Check:** render-and-compare from `4VkQ…`, moved 4.4 m back, puts the hotel edge, windows, vents and the 鴻勝館 sign on their photographed positions.
