# Lorong 12 south to Guillemard Road

Research and implementation from **22 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 96. This brief covers `src/lorong-12-south-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Lorong 12's east side from No. 21 (modelled under question 92) south to Guillemard Road, the lane's twelve southern ways, and Guillemard Road's way from Lorong 12 to Lorong 14, which joins the stretch taken with Lorongs 14 and 16.
- **Source:** the dated extension `public/osm-extension.osm` (question 81), taken in by id.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drive of 19 May 2023 up the lane from Guillemard Road (sequence 8344969, frames 528-546).
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±2 m.
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (question 84); legible signs are recorded below.
- **Left for later:** the yards behind the east side (ways 454438315, 764372590 and 764372586), and the lane's west side south of the old edge, which the extension leaves without outlines facing the lane.
