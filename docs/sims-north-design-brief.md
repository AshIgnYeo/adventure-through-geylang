## How dimensions were measured

- a camera distance of **8.96 m** (GPS: 9.53 m), when scaled to the 11.02 m combined source frontage;
- a **6.36 m** grey frontage and a **4.66 m** white frontage. The source ways are an equal **5.51 m + 5.51 m**.

The satellite overlay is consistent with roofs that do not follow the equal source split. Each building's volume, front and roof therefore follow its measured span. The source outlines and collision are unchanged, so the white No. 133 identity stands 0.85 m inside its own source way.

## What is modelled

- **No. 131:** the grey two-storey front; two brown-glazed upper windows with louvred vents; the fascia, redrawn originally in red on cream; a five-foot way with an opaque shop opening; and a flat roof.
- **No. 133:** the white front; three tall dark-framed upper windows with transom lights; the large signboard, with a redrawn dragon roundel and serif lettering; a small QIANJING CRYSTAL sign over opaque shop glazing; a grey side pilaster; and an estimated tiled gable roof behind the tiled eave.

## What is omitted or estimated

- **Not modelled:** stock, crystal displays, the FRISco logo, the parked car, bins, cones, wiring and the interiors. The dragon roundel is a simplification.
- **Estimates:** the five-foot way depth (1.6 m), the No. 133 roof form and the window divisions.
