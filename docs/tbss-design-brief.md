# Tibetan Buddhist Studies Society 藏密佛学研究会, 21 Lorong 11 Geylang

Research and implementation: **10 October 2026**. Q45, on the owner-approved re-spaced Lorong 11 west row (open question 1).

## Evidence and assignment

- **Signage, April 2024:** panoramas `64XRklcWkCBOa9KyrvJ_PA` and `73intX-f7jQoMaTMSJ_4Mg` show the white conserved shophouse with teal shutters at No. 21, between Goodwood (No. 19) and the plain No. 23. It carries:
  - a red board reading **藏密佛学研究会 / Tibetan Buddhist Studies Society**, with TBSS.ORG and a mandala;
  - a ledge above it holding a gilded **Dharma wheel flanked by two deer**, with vases and dragon finials.
- **Identity source:** no Google Maps listing was found. The identity rests on the society's signage at a sourced street address, recorded in the queue on 9 October 2026.
- **Footprint:** the frontage measures about **5.2 m**. The house stands on the authored footprint `l11w-21`, which replaces source way [1223407883](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1223407883) (6.39 m).

## How it was measured

- **Elevation:** the upper storey is a twin of No. 19 and uses the shared measurements in `peranakan-layout.mjs`. The board (1.15–3.97 m along, 3.39–4.1 m high), the ornament ledge (4.47–4.53 m) and the ornament positions were read at full resolution. The awning was read from the square-on frame.
- **Render-and-compare:** the board, wheel, deer and north party line overlay the photograph. The generic No. 23 beyond it now meets the photographed pilaster.

## What is modelled and omitted

- **Modelled:**
  - the shared Peranakan upper storey;
  - the ornament ledge with the gilded wheel, deer, vases and finials;
  - the red board, redrawn originally, with an approximate Tibetan-script line and a simplified mandala;
  - the red awning;
  - a glazed screen and an altar cabinet in the five-foot way.
- **Not modelled:** the thangka artwork and the mandala's detail, vehicles, plants and the interior.
- **Approximate:** the Tibetan line is a stand-in and is not a transcription of the board.
