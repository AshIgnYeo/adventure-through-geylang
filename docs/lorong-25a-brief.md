# Lorong 25A

Research and implementation from **28 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 107. This brief covers `src/lorong-25a-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Lorong 25A from Sims Avenue to Geylang Road, both sides: eleven outlines, with the lane's fifteen ways drawn southward from Sims Avenue as a walkable corridor.
- **Source:** the Lorong 25 extract, `public/osm-extension-lorong-25.osm` (103.8822-103.8834 E, 1.3131-1.3158 N, retrieved 28 November 2026), which already holds the whole lane. The Geylang Road corner east of the lane was also held by the east extension, which never took it in.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drive of 16 May 2023 down the lane (sequence 8344809, frames 2066-2091).
- **Matching:** the source outlines here are coarser than the frontages. One outline holds a temple and a row of link houses; another a modern house, an apartment block and part of a building site. Each outline takes the frontages its corners bracket in the frames, with upper volumes where one part stands taller. The drive's positions trail by about 10 m, so this carries some uncertainty (open question 108).
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±2 m (±4 m for five storeys or more).
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (question 84), its boards drawn blank.

## Status, 28 November 2026

**Complete,** with two gaps the source leaves: a pair of ornate shophouses and a modern house between Centra Suites and the apartment block have no outline of their own, and a small block behind the ECM Building (way 454254135) faces only the service alley. Both are part of question 108.
