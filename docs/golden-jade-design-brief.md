# Golden Jade Restaurant, 271 Geylang Road

Research and implementation: **7 October 2026**. Q21, added in this session and built at the user's direct request.

## Evidence and assignment

- **Current listing:** Google Maps, read on 7 October 2026, lists **Golden Jade Restaurant 金翠餐厅, 271 Geylang Rd, Singapore 389324**, open, with telephone 6744 3022. [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/2630612/golden-jade) gives the same address.
- **Street View:** June 2024 panorama `jYF-BzVq__RpGZWD3Tplwg`, at the Lorong 13 corner, shows the black marquee signboard reading **金翠餐厅 GOLDEN JADE RESTAURANT**, **SINCE 2003** and **271**.
- **Assignment:** the neighbour to the west is the Lorong 13 corner shophouse, signed BN Food Palace. Google Maps lists BN Food Palace at **No. 269** and marks it **permanently closed**. The neighbour to the east is signed Zui Xiang (醉香楼). Counting 5.13 m frontages east from No. 269, and west from the Buddhist Art Centre at No. 285, both identify unnumbered [way 454254220](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/454254220) as No. 271. Only that way is assigned. The closed corner unit stays generic.
- **Stale source point:** the retained 2017 point named Golden Jade Restaurant lies in the corner way 454254219, one unit west of the signed frontage. It is recorded but not used.

## What is modelled

The upper storey follows the shared row elevation, measured at [Eat First](eat-first-design-brief.md) and also used for [RR Motor](rr-motor-design-brief.md), in the jalousie finish. It has three arched openings with white jalousies, brown arch heads and trim, relief cartouches and frieze, and a brown eaves board.

The roof is estimated and uses the shared geometry. It is finished in weathered clay tiles with grey-green coping caps, as seen from the corner.

At street level:

- **Signboard:** the black marquee signboard, redrawn with original typography and a border of bulbs. It is mounted proud of the cornice, as photographed.
- **Pictures:** a strip of eight blank picture frames, with no food images reproduced.
- **Back wall:** a closed roller shutter and a dark display door.

## What is omitted or estimated

- **Not modelled:** menu boards, food pictures, the small certification badge on the sign, the vertical Zui Xiang sign belonging to the neighbour, cables and any interior.
- **Estimates:** the roof form and heights follow the measured row. The sign and shutter positions are read from the corner panorama. The five-foot way depth is assumed to match No. 287.
