# Lorong 22, east side

Research and implementation from **28 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 104. This brief covers `src/lorong-22-east-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Lorong 22's east side from the Geylang Road corner to the Guillemard Road corner, seventeen plots, with the lane's southbound carriageway.
- **Source:** a narrow dated extract, `public/osm-extension-east.osm` (103.8822-103.8832 E, 1.3095-1.3135 N, retrieved 28 November 2026), read after the retained extract and the first extension, so each earlier file keeps its versions. Only this lane's ways and outlines are taken from it, by id.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drive of 16 May 2023 up the lane's northbound carriageway (sequence 8344809, frames 1035-1060), which faces the east side across both carriageways.
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±2 m (±4 m for the towers).
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (question 84). Many source ways carry names; they are recorded below with what the frames show.

## Status, 28 November 2026

**Complete.** Lorong 22 is now walked on both sides.
