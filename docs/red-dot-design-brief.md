# Red Dot Running Company, 108 Sims Avenue

Research and implementation: **10 October 2026**. Q53, on the owner-approved re-spaced Sims Avenue row (open question 8a).

## Evidence and assignment

- **Listings:** Google Maps (about 213 reviews) and Time Out list **Red Dot Running Company**, a running store, at **108 Sims Avenue, Singapore 387432**, open.
- **Street View, June 2024:** panoramas `f3PA…`, `dlOj…` and `YsOo…` show its shopfront glazing with mountain-silhouette decals, red-dot logos and running-wear mannequins. A large **108** is lettered on the central pier. The shop is in the pale-pink three-storey Art Deco row west of the Amrise Hotel.

## The re-spacing, and a correction

- **Earlier reading:** the open-questions log proposed re-spacing Nos. 106–112 as four uniform 7.1 m bays, read from the upper storeys.
- **Ground-floor evidence:** ground-floor frames rectified 1.5 m in front of the source front, where the 108 pier aligns across three panoramas, show something different. Measured along the front from the Fok Wai Kee corner:
  - the pier lettered 108 stands on the source 108/110 party line, at **20.76 m**;
  - the pier against Amrise stands on the source 110/112 line, at **27.17 m**;
  - only the 106/108 pier is off, at about **15.0 m** against the source 12.45 m.
- **Applied:** the 106/108 line moves to 15.0 m, and the former Nos. 108 and 110 east of it form one unit, `sa-108`, 12.17 m wide. Red Dot's shopfront runs that whole width, across the 108 pier.
  - No. 106 becomes `sa-106`, 8.71 m, and stays generic.
  - Amrise and Fok Wai Kee keep their source ways.
- **Merge helper:** the pieces are joined with a shared-edge merge helper that drops near-collinear vertices.
- **Superseded:** the upper-storey four-bay reading is not borne out at ground level and is superseded. The open-questions entry has been updated.
