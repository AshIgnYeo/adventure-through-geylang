# Lorong 21A north of Sims Avenue

Research and implementation from **14 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 89. This brief covers `src/lorong-21a-north-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Lorong 21A from Sims Avenue north-west to about 12 m past the extract's north edge, far enough to take in its plots' fronts. Five outlines stand along it inside the extract.
- **Map:** `scripts/prepare-map.mjs` takes the five buildings and the lane's three ways beyond its mouth by id; the lane is a walkable corridor from the avenue's centreline.
- **Imagery and modelling:** as for [Lorongs 19 and 21 north](lorong-19-21-north-brief.md): KartaView's drive of 16 May 2023 (sequence 8344809, frames 3735-3758) runs up and down the lane, and the plots are photographed massing on their source outlines, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±4 m for the towers.
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (open question 84); legible signs and source names are recorded below.

## Status, 14 November 2026

**Complete.** All five plots are modelled as massing.
