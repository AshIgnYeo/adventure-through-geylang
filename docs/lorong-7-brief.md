# Lorong 7 at the extract's west edge

Research and implementation from **14 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 94. This brief covers `src/lorong-7-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Lorong 7 from Geylang Road's back lane north to Sims Avenue, on the extract's west edge, and Sims Avenue's way west of the study rectangle. Its east side was already in the map (Centra Residence and the blocks south of it); this walk takes in the five plots that were not.
- **Map:** `scripts/prepare-map.mjs` takes the five buildings, the lane's three ways (666812086, 634186084 and 666812085) and Sims Avenue's way 633797716. The source's lane stops about 50 m short of Sims Avenue, where it leaves the extract, so its corridor runs on 36 m along the line of its last way to reach Winsome Apartments.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drives of 16, 17 and 19 May 2023 down the lane (sequences 8344809, 8344873 and 8344969), the 17 May drive's pass along Lorong Tahar, and the 24 May drive along Sims Avenue (8348649).
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, as on the lanes north of Sims Avenue, with heights from storey counts and elevation angles carrying about ±2 m.
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (open question 84); legible signs and source names are recorded below.
- **Left out:** JCUS Block C (way 304613525) at the extract's north edge, which no lane in the map reaches.

## Status, 14 November 2026

**Complete.** All five plots are modelled as massing.
