# Sui Yuan Ju Buddhist Society, 2–4 Lorong 13 Geylang

Research and implementation: **7 October 2026**. Q25, added in this session and built at the user's direct request.

## Evidence and assignment

- **Current listing:** Google Maps, read on 7 October 2026, lists **随缘居佛社（精舍）Sui Yuan Ju Buddhist Society, 4 Lor 13 Geylang, Singapore 388642**, with current opening hours and a telephone number. A general web search found no other listing.
- **Street View:** April 2024 is the latest capture.
  - Panorama `FXTw5jMQr6bKoFFGgqyVkw` is opposite No. 2, and `f-IHSXdP88-BA3LwDO6R0Q` is opposite No. 4's north end.
  - Over No. 4 is the yellow **随缘居佛社 (精舍) Sui Yuan Ju Buddhist Society** signboard, giving **No4, Lor 13 Geylang Road**.
  - A continuous red awning runs on across **No. 2**, where a dark-tiled Chinese canopy roof shelters timber doors with red couplets, flanked by stone lions, with an incense urn in front.
- **Assignment:** retained ways [1223454593](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1223454593), tagged **No. 4**, and [1223454592](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1223454592), tagged **No. 2**, stand side by side on Lorong 13 at the Geylang Road end.

Both are assigned to one landmark as a **provisional two-unit visual assignment**, following the precedent of Leong Kee. The name and address belong to No. 4. No. 2 is not separately listed. Its use by the society is suggested by the shared awning and shrine porch, but is not asserted. The modern No. 6A to the north remains generic.

## How dimensions were measured

The panorama GPS positions disagreed with the façade by 1.5–3 m, so the façade was located by **triangulating between the two panoramas**, which are about 10 m apart.

- **Unit widths:** in a 120° square-on frame tilted 22° upwards from the No. 2 panorama, the No. 4 north edge, the central pilaster and the No. 2 south edge give unit widths of about **5.94 m and 5.68 m**. That matches the equal **5.81 m** source split within measurement error. An early reading that suggested unequal units was a misidentified pilaster.
- **Camera distance:** the same frame puts the camera about **6.3 m** from the façade.
- **Camera height:** a fire hydrant and the kerb line show the camera stood unusually low, about **1.35 m** above the ground. For the No. 4 panorama, the window and sill heights give a camera about **1.85 m** high. The signboard, beam and awning were measured from that panorama.

## What is modelled

- **Upper storeys:** both are two-storey and ornate.
  - Three segmental-arched windows per unit, under pink and cream archivolts with diamond-lattice timber fanlights. No. 4 has red-brown shutters with green-glazed upper casements; No. 2 has brown louvred shutters.
  - Cream pilasters with pink fillets, green tile strips with pink flowers and green-and-pink acanthus capitals. The central pilaster and pier are shared and split at the party line.
  - Glazed floral tile panels under the windows, and a pink frieze of green leaf scrolls with round vents over the arches.
  - A red timber fretwork fascia with a scalloped fringe, cut out against the dark soffit.
- **No. 4, ground floor:** the yellow signboard, redrawn with original typography; a red awning with valance; draped altar tables; and red couplet boards beside a back-wall doorway.
- **No. 2, ground floor:** the dark-tiled hipped canopy with a ridge and end ornaments; a red awning below it; and timber grilled double doors with red couplet boards.
- **Five-foot ways and roof:** a 1.8 m five-foot way (estimated) with a red-carpeted floor before No. 2, and an estimated tiled gable roof behind the fascia.

## What is omitted or estimated

- **Omitted:** the stone lions, incense urn, altar furnishings and offerings, couplet wording, framed pictures, lanterns, flowers, plastic stools, ladders, bins and the interior. The canopy's slight overhang past No. 2's south edge is also left out.
- **Estimates:** the five-foot way depth, roof form, ridge-ornament shapes, door and table layouts, and the tile and frieze patterns, which are original redrawings.
