# Guillemard Road between Lorongs 16 and 20

Research and implementation from **22 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 100. This brief covers `src/guillemard-road-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Guillemard Road between Lorong 16 and Lorong 20: the row of houses on its north side west of the Lorong 20 corner (Nos. 175-181), the six outlines on its south side (Nos. 84-94), and both carriageways between the two lanes, with a corridor along each.
- **Source:** the dated extension `public/osm-extension.osm` (question 81), taken in by id. This is the first time the map reaches across Guillemard Road.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drive of 19 May 2023 east along the road (sequence 8344969, frames 810-818).
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±2 m.
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (question 84).
- **Left for later:** the two-storey house further west on the north side (way 454274623), seen only at the edge of the frames, and the rest of Guillemard Road's south side.
