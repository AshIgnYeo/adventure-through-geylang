Research and implementation from **10 October 2026**, after Lorong 13 (open question 19). This brief covers the Lorong 11 places in `src/lorong-11-layout.mjs`. Places built earlier in their own modules keep their own briefs.

Unnamed places (open question 18) carry no business name and leave their boards blank. Heights are above the five-foot-way floor, which stands about 0.5 m above the road on this row.

## No. 15 (unnamed)

- **Footprint:** the re-spaced No. 15 footprint (open question 1), `l11w-15`, 9.3 m wide. It is the first house of the west row, and its outline wraps behind Hotel 81 Joy's yard. The roof spans to the deepest corner at each end of the front, so the wrapped outline gets one roof.
- **Building:** a cream, two-bay, conserved Peranakan shophouse with:
  - five windows with louvred shutters and pierced fretwork fanlights under arched heads, the fourth a full-height French window;
  - floral tile panels and tiled pilaster strips;
  - a deep moulded cornice;
  - a wide central pier.
- **Identity:** no listing or sign was found, so it is unnamed.
- **Measurement:** read on `H9_p…` rectified at the row's 0.6 m façade depth. Allowing for the 0.5 m raised walkway, the heights match the elevation measured at Nos. 19 and 21. That elevation is therefore shared, and its layout now takes per-house window, pilaster and pier positions.
- **Check:** render-and-compare from the `H9_p…` pose, moved 0.6 m back for the façade depth, puts the five windows, the central pier and the ground-floor openings on their photographed positions.
