# Sik Wai Sin frontage, 289 Geylang Road

Research and implementation: **7 October 2026**. Q19, added in this session after Eat First (Q16) and built at the user's direct request.

## Evidence and assignment

- **ACRA-derived records:** [RecordOwl](https://recordowl.com/company/sik-wai-sin-eating-house) and [ACRAWatch](https://acrawatch.sg/company/sik-wai-sin-eating-house) list **Sik Wai Sin Eating House** at **289 Geylang Road #01-289, Singapore 389336**. The entity was registered in 1979 and changed its address on **6 September 2024**.
- **Google Maps:** read on 7 October 2026, it places Sik Wai Sin Eating House at **287** Geylang Road with Eat First's telephone number. That reflects the family business's return to No. 287, described in the [Eat First brief](eat-first-design-brief.md).
- **Street View:** June 2024 remains the latest capture. Panorama `4E9yml2h1kTBeHbJ3p0Ygg`, square to the frontage, shows the red **食為先 SIK WAI SIN** fascia numbered **289** and a small **食為先大飯店 SIK WAI SIN EATING HOUSE** board over a closed shutter.

This is therefore a **dated June 2024 exterior** of the signed Sik Wai Sin frontage. Whether No. 289 trades, or under what name, after June 2024 is unresolved. The model makes no claim about current use, in the same way as the SHG Engineering exterior.

Unnumbered [way 454254229](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/454254229) is the next 5.13 m frontage east of Eat First. It is about 14.72 m deep and shares Eat First's east corners. Only this way is assigned. All source coordinates are unchanged. No. 291 remains generic.

## How it was built

Nos. 287 and 289 share one elevation. The [Eat First brief](eat-first-design-brief.md) records how its heights were measured photogrammetrically. They include the roof ridge about 8 m back; the triangulated No. 289/291 coping peak at 7.7 m back and 12.5 m high agrees. The common geometry now lives in a shared paired-shophouse module used by both units.

## What is modelled

- **Upper storey and roof:** identical to No. 287: three segmental-arched green casements with red frames between Corinthian pilasters, relief cartouches and frieze, the green gutter, and an orange tiled gable roof with raised copings.
- **Fascia:** red, redrawn with original typography reading `食為先`, `SIK WAI SIN` and `289`. It carries a simplified round emblem: the character 食 in a white ring with a tasselled cord.
- **Five-foot way:** tiled floor, white ceiling and tube light. On the back wall are the dark timber double door, the small red board and a closed aluminium shutter under its hood.
- **Piers:** brick-faced, with courses and consoles.

## What is omitted or estimated

- **Lettering:** the vertical red characters on the east pier are omitted, because the fourth is hidden in every view. The emblem's fine detail is simplified.
- **Not modelled:** the wall lamp, cables, CCTV box, wire hooks, bin and any interior.
- **Downpipe:** No. 287 already carries the shared green downpipe, so No. 289 has none of its own.
- **Neighbours:** the five-foot way is closed by thin returns at both ends. No. 291 is generic and lower, so No. 289's east gable shows above it.
- **Review starts:** the study's walkable boundary runs just west of this frontage's centre. Both starts therefore look across from in front of No. 287.
