# Eat First, 287 Geylang Road

Research and implementation: **7 October 2026**. Q16 only, following clean commit `949dc49`. Built at the user's direct request in this session.

## Evidence and assignment

A web search on 7 October 2026 returned [Eatbook](https://eatbook.sg/eat-first/) and [Burpple](https://www.burpple.com/eat-first) listings for **Eat First, 287 Geylang Road**. [ieatishootipost's August 2023 update](https://ieatishootipost.sg/eat-first-tale-two-brothers/) records the restaurant's July 2023 return to the original Sik Wai Sin premises at this number. These establish a published restaurant address, not surveyed boundaries or whole-building occupation.

Retained [OSM node 4689499460](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/4689499460) at 103.8782335, 1.3125548 is named Sik Wai Sin (Kaart ground survey, 2017) and lies inside [way 454254228](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/454254228). The way carries no address tags. Its street edge is approximately **5.13 m** wide and the outline about **14.72 m** deep. The Buddhist Art Centre node lies in the next way west, which fixes the unit order. All original coordinates and the whole-building collision polygon remain unchanged.

Google Street View from **June 2024** is the latest capture on this stretch. Panorama metadata lists earlier captures back to 2008 and none later. Three June 2024 panoramas were inspected directly:

- [Panorama `LiyhSflRbRfon_vCcaecGQ`](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=LiyhSflRbRfon_vCcaecGQ&heading=352&pitch=10&fov=90) stands in front of No. 285 and gives straight-on views of the No. 287 five-foot way and upper storey.
- [Panorama `4E9yml2h1kTBeHbJ3p0Ygg`](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=4E9yml2h1kTBeHbJ3p0Ygg&heading=352&pitch=0&fov=90) stands in front of No. 289 and shows the pier shared with No. 289.
- [Panorama `_w5yHraTw78BWJJ4wn1RSQ`](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=_w5yHraTw78BWJJ4wn1RSQ&heading=28&pitch=9&fov=34), at the Lorong 12 corner across the road, shows the full elevation and the roof.

The fascia reads **食之為鮮 EAT FIRST** with **287**. The next fascia east reads **食為先 SIK WAI SIN**.

Only way 454254228 is assigned. No. 289 remains generic, and a separate check is still needed before Sik Wai Sin is assigned there.

## How dimensions were measured

The Street View images were treated as pinhole views. Rotating the heading moved a downpipe to within 2 px of its predicted position, which confirms the field of view.

- **Straight-on views:** with the view square to the façade (heading 352°), the 5.13 m frontage gives a camera distance of **6.36 m**. A pedestrian's height and the five-foot way floor give a camera height of about **2.4 m**. Heights from frames tilted 0° and 30° upwards agree to within 0.08 m.
- **Five-foot way depth:** the dark door in the five-foot way was located from two panoramas. Its edge falls on the No. 287/289 party line about **2.0 m** behind the piers, so the door belongs to No. 289 and is excluded.
- **Roof:** the cross-road camera was calibrated against measured window and downpipe positions, with a fit error of about 9 px. Its rays to the peaks of the party-wall copings give a ridge about **8 m** back at roughly **12.2 m** to the tiles. That is about a 29° pitch. The next coping east gives a matching 7.7 m and 12.5 m.

The measured heights are listed below. They carry roughly ±0.15 m of uncertainty.

## What is modelled

- **Massing:** two storeys under an orange clay-tile gable roof, its ridge parallel to Geylang Road about 8 m back. The raised party-wall copings end in blocks above the gutter. The eaves line and green gutter continue No. 285's.
- **Upper storey:** three equal openings, centred 1.5 m apart, between four Corinthian pilasters with leaf and volute capitals. Each opening has a red frame, a transom and a segmental fanlight with white glazing bars, under a white archivolt. The two green casement leaves each have four panes over a white louvred panel.
- **Plasterwork:** a continuous sill course; three elongated octagonal cartouches with mirrored leaf scrolls; a running scroll frieze; and cornice mouldings under the green fascia and gutter. The relief is drawn as an original shaded pattern, not copied from the reference.
- **Signs:** the red fascia, redrawn with original typography reading `食之為鮮`, `EAT FIRST` and `287`, with a simplified black fish-head logo. On the back wall, a maroon board carries silver `食之為鮮` and `EAT FIRST` with the same logo. A small `287` plate sits on the inner face of the west pier.
- **Five-foot way:** a 2.0 m deep tiled floor, white ceiling with a tube light and white piers with consoles. The west pier abuts No. 285's ochre pier; the east pier is No. 287's half of the pier shared with No. 289.
- **Back wall:** an aluminium-framed glass shopfront, kept opaque, and a grille gate with a dark barred transom.
- **Downpipe:** the green downpipe on the No. 287/289 party line, with its offset to the gutter.

## What is omitted or estimated

- **Not modelled:** the dining room and everything behind the glass, diners, staff, menus, notices on the glass, red paper banners and seasonal decorations, the parked cars, overhead wiring, and the speaker and light fittings on the beam.
- **No. 289's five-foot way:** its dark timber door and brick-faced pier half are left out.
- **Unseen parts:** the rear slope falls to the rear wall at eaves height. The rear slope, rear wall and party walls above the neighbouring roofs are simple massing, not a survey.
- **Estimates:** the fish logo is a simplification. Relief motifs, capital ornament, louvre counts, glazing-bar spacing and colours are estimates from the panoramas.
- **Five-foot way ends:** these are closed by thin returns. The real five-foot way is continuous.
- **Roof heights:** the generic neighbour at No. 289 is lower, so No. 287's east gable shows above it. No. 287's roof also rises well above the estimated No. 285 roof. The attic structure behind No. 285 (see that brief) appears at about No. 287's ridge height in the cross-road view.

- source-coordinate retention and containment of the Sik Wai Sin point;
- the Buddhist Art Centre and No. 289 assignments;
- fittings inside the frontage and five-foot way, and clearance of the Geylang Road carriageway;
- windows between the pilasters and below the frieze;
- roof coverage, the triangulated ridge depth, the eaves meeting the shared fascia, and upward normals;
- walkable, correctly aimed review starts.
