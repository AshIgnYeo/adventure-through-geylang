# Shophouse atlas

Tool: built-in image generation. Output: `public/shophouse-atlas.png`.

Prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: building façade texture atlas for a realistic mobile 3D Singapore street game. Create one square image, a seamless horizontal row of FOUR different adjoining two-storey early Singapore Geylang shophouse front elevations, each occupying precisely one quarter of the image width. Camera perfectly orthographic straight-on, no perspective, no street, no sky, no ground, full rectangular facade image edge to edge. Roof parapet at very top, upper storey timber louvred shutters and plaster pilasters in upper half; horizontal blank signboard band at 52 percent height; dark open shop entrance and metal folding grilles bottom half. Off-white, muted sage, faded ochre, pale terracotta plaster with subtle weather stains, ceramic tile plinths, believable fine masonry detail. Uniform neutral overcast light, minimal shadows, no cast shadow across adjacent facades. Four units share floor heights, but varied shutters and minor ornament. No people, vehicles, business names, logos, letters, text, modern glass towers, watermarks. Architectural texture, not a concept scene. Must feel real, imperfect, lived in, moderately maintained, no exaggerated decay.

Review: usable as an illustrative texture atlas, but more ornate than the area's early-style heritage frontage. It must not be treated as a replica of a specific real building.
