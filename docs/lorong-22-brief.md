# Lorong 22, west side

Research and implementation from **22 November 2026**, under open questions 84 and 98. This brief covers `src/lorong-22-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** Lorong 22's west side from Guillemard Road to Geylang Road: the back half of the block shared with Lorong 20, whose plots stand 13-30 m back from the lane behind yards, car parks and fences.
- **Source:** the dated extension `public/osm-extension.osm` (question 81), which reaches 103.8822 E and so takes in the lane's four ways but none of its east side.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drive of 16 May 2023 up the lane (sequence 8344809, frames 1036-1060), matched to the plots by their order along the lane.
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±2 m (±4 m for the towers). The Karma Kagyud Buddhist Centre's outline is irregular, so its front is laid on its longest lane-facing edge.
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (question 84); legible lettering is recorded below.
- **Left for later:** the lane's east side, which needs an extract reaching further east.
