## What is modelled

- **Upper storey:** a white conserved two-storey frontage with two tall windows and a central French window, all with black frames, tinted glass and head mouldings.
- **Pilasters and tiles:** slender pilasters with capitals and volutes. The capitals of the shared party pilasters stop at the party line. Pink-red floral tile panels sit under the side windows.
- **Eaves:** the white timber fretwork valance shared along the row, cut out with pointed teeth, over a dark soffit.
- **Ground floor:**
  - the black signboard and the blade sign of three discs, both redrawn originally;
  - piers with consoles and a mosaic-coloured five-foot way;
  - two display windows, glazed double doors with brass pulls, and the number 7 plate.
- **Roof:** an estimated tiled gable roof.

## What is omitted or estimated

- **Not modelled:** the brass bins and urn at the door, the parking signpost, the vending machine and banners next door, and the interior.
- **Estimates:** the five-foot way depth, back-wall heights, roof form and tile pattern.
