# Lorongs 14 and 16 south to Guillemard Road

Research and implementation from **22 November 2026**, under open questions 52, 62 and 84. This brief covers `src/lorong-14-16-south-layout.mjs`.

## Scope and method

- **Stretch:** the south ends of Lorong 14 (from No. 37 on its east side) and Lorong 16 (from No. 54 and the terrace at the head of Westerhout Road) to Guillemard Road, with Guillemard Road's north carriageway between the two lanes.
- **Source:** the dated extension `public/osm-extension.osm` (question 81). Lorong 14's six southern ways, Lorong 16's three, and Guillemard Road's two ways between them come in by id, with corridors along each.
- **Imagery:** KartaView's drive of 19 May 2023 (sequence 8344969), which runs down Lorong 14 three times (frames 6-24, 100-118 and 688-706) and up Lorong 16 once (frames 714-723).
- **Modelling:** photographed massing on the source outlines, with heights from storey counts carrying about ±2 m (±4 m for blocks of seven storeys or more).
- **Naming:** every building is unnamed for now (question 84); legible signs, and the listings questions 52 and 62 recorded in November, are noted below.
- **Lorong 14's west side** south of the old edge is Wing Fong Mansions, already modelled over its whole outline with the Lorong 14 walk. Its lane face can now be walked to its south end.

## Status, 22 November 2026

**Complete.** All thirteen plots are modelled as massing.
